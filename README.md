## 🏗️ Featured Projects

### 🏨 Houda Analytics — Hotel Revenue Intelligence Platform
> `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `React` `RAG` `LLM Agents` `ETL` — *Freelance @ Houda Hotels Tunisia*

A full-stack revenue intelligence platform for hotel managers. Plugs into booking & financial data, runs ETL pipelines, detects anomalies, simulates revenue scenarios, and explains KPI shifts in plain English through a conversational AI agent. Because spreadsheets are so 2010.

---

### 📻 Radio AI — Fully Agentic Radio Platform
> `LangChain` `RAG` `TTS` `Avatar Animation` `Lip-sync` — *Internship @ Vision Age VFX*

Designed a multi-agent workflow where AI writes the scripts, a virtual host reads them out loud, and an animated avatar delivers them live — with program scheduling, human validation, and content archiving built in. Yes, it's as cool as it sounds.

---

### 🎓 Routiny — Personalized Study & Wellbeing Platform
> `FastAPI` `React` `LangGraph` `LangChain` `Vector DB`

An AI learning companion that adapts to your pace, mental load, and performance in real time. Multi-agent backend (LangGraph) handles everything from study planning to emotional check-ins. RAG retrieves course content for contextual explanations.

---

### 🛒 Hybrid Recommendation Engine — PrestaShop
> `Content-Based Filtering` `Collaborative Filtering` `PHP` `jQuery` — *Internship @ Best Pack*

Built for an e-commerce platform selling traditional Tunisian products internationally. Recommends related products dynamically to boost cross-selling — because serendipity should be engineered.
